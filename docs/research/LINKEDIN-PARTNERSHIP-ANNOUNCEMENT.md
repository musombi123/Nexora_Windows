# LinkedIn announcement — NEXORA open for partnership

**Recommended public copy**

I'm building **NEXORA Interactive Studios**, with **Nexora Foundry** as our flagship product: a creator-led, local-first platform designed to bring more of the game-development workflow into one guided workspace.

The idea is to help creators move from a structured game brief to procedural worlds and assets, Godot project assembly, validation and export—while keeping project files and production workloads on the creator's own computer.

The engineering foundation brings together a React/TypeScript creator workspace, a FastAPI production core, a paired desktop Local Agent, Blender-based procedural workflows and Godot 4. The Windows desktop host is built with Tauri 2, and its installer pipeline is still undergoing validation.

We are still developing and validating the product. This is not a claim that every workflow is production-ready or that NEXORA replaces established game engines. Godot remains the first supported engine, and creator testing, device acceptance and release hardening are active priorities.

**I’m open to conversations with potential partners and collaborators** in:
- Godot and game-development tooling;
- 3D art, technical art, animation and asset pipelines;
- game design, creator education and training;
- QA, hardware testing and creator communities;
- publishing, distribution and ecosystem development;
- applied research and strategic partnerships.

I’m especially interested in practical collaborations where we can define a clear problem, agree on a measurable pilot, and build something that can be tested—not just make an announcement.

If your work aligns with this direction, let's connect. You can reach me through LinkedIn messages to discuss a possible partnership with NEXORA Interactive Studios.
